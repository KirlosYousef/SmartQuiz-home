Can I make a quiz about any topic?+

Yes. Choose one of the suggested categories or enter your own custom topic to build a quiz around something you want to explore.

What kinds of questions can I play?+

Choose from question styles such as multiple choice, true or false, flashcards, and explain-the-answer. Available choices can depend on your quiz setup.

How do flashcards work?+

When you miss an answer, you can save it as a flashcard and review it later. Your flashcard deck is organized by topic.

Is SmartQuiz free to download?+

SmartQuiz is free on the App Store and offers optional in-app purchases. Any subscription details and prices are shown in the app before purchase.
