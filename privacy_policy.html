Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the SmartQuiz iOS app and this website handle information. It explains what is stored on your device, what is sent to service providers when you use a feature, and how to contact us with a privacy request.

Effective October 12, 2026 · Updated October 12, 2026

SmartQuiz does not require you to create an account in the current iOS app. Some app activity is saved locally; information is sent to a provider when you use AI quiz generation, analytics, purchases, or feedback features.

Who we are

SmartQuiz (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is an educational quiz app developed by Kirlos Yousef. This policy applies to the SmartQuiz app and www.smartquiz.kirlosyousef.com.

Information we handle

Information saved on your device

The app stores the preferences and learning progress needed to provide its features. Depending on how you use the app, this may include a display name, selected interests, quiz language and settings, question history, flashcards, streak and milestone progress, points, and reminder preferences. Daily reminders are scheduled through iOS notifications.

Information sent to generate quizzes

When you ask SmartQuiz to make a quiz, the app sends the topic or custom topic you entered, quiz settings such as difficulty, language, and question style, optional instructions, and recent question history or learning-journey context when needed to make the quiz. These details are sent to OpenAI, the AI service used by the app, which returns questions and feedback for display. Do not include sensitive or confidential information in a topic or instruction. OpenAI handles requests under its own terms and privacy practices; we do not make claims here about its retention periods.

Analytics and app activity

SmartQuiz uses Mixpanel to understand app usage and improve the experience. The app's analytics implementation sends events such as app opens, screens viewed, onboarding choices, and quiz activity. Event details can include the selected topic, quiz settings, language, app version, and subscription state. If you have entered a display name, the app may use it as the analytics profile identifier and name. Mixpanel may also process technical and device information as described in its own documentation and privacy notice.

Purchases and feedback

If you make an in-app purchase, Apple and RevenueCat process purchase and entitlement information so the app can provide the subscription or purchase you selected. The app may also provide a display name to RevenueCat. If you submit a feature suggestion, SmartQuiz uses WishKit to receive and display the request; the suggestion and the display name you choose to share may be processed by WishKit. These providers handle data under their own terms and policies.

Website visits

This website is a static site hosted through GitHub Pages. It does not currently include an analytics tracker, advertising pixel, account, or form. GitHub may process basic request and security information to provide its hosting service under GitHub's privacy terms.

How information is used

Information is used to generate the quiz you request, save your learning progress and preferences, operate optional purchase and feedback features, understand product usage, maintain and improve the app, and respond to support or privacy requests.

Service providers and sharing

We use the providers described above only as relevant to the feature you use. They may process information on our behalf or under their own privacy terms. We do not sell personal information. Information may also be disclosed if required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the service, its users, or legal rights.

AI quiz generation and other online features require an internet connection. Providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live, subject to their own safeguards and legal obligations.

Storage and retention

Learning preferences and progress are stored by the app on your device. Analytics, AI requests, purchases, and feature suggestions may be handled by their respective providers. Their retention is governed by their own policies and settings. We do not claim a specific provider retention period in this policy.

We use reasonable measures appropriate to this app to protect information under our control. Internet transmission and electronic storage cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices and privacy requests

  • You choose whether to enter a display name, enable reminders, submit a feature suggestion, or use AI quiz generation.
  • You can change app preferences in SmartQuiz or in iOS Settings where applicable. You can turn off notification permission in iOS Settings.
  • Deleting SmartQuiz from your device removes its locally stored app data, subject to any iOS backup behavior. This does not automatically delete information already handled by Apple or another service provider.
  • For a question, access request, correction request, or deletion request involving information we control, email hello@kirlosyousef.com. We may need enough information to understand and respond to the request. For provider-held purchase, analytics, or AI service information, the provider may need to handle the request directly.

Children's privacy

SmartQuiz is an educational app available through the App Store. Parents or guardians who have questions about a child's use of the app or information entered into a quiz request can contact us at hello@kirlosyousef.com. Please do not enter sensitive personal information into a quiz topic or custom instruction.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. We will post the current version on this page and update the effective date above.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@kirlosyousef.com.
